About NJC Filmworks

Crafting cinematic stories since day one

Every frame is a
deliberate choice.

NJC Filmworks was built on a single belief: that the most meaningful moments in life deserve to be preserved with the same care and artistry as a feature film.

From intimate backyard weddings to large-scale brand campaigns, every project receives the same level of creative investment — meticulous lighting, intentional camera work, and an edit that makes you feel something.

Based on the East Coast and available for travel anywhere, NJC Filmworks brings a cinematic eye to wherever the story takes us.
